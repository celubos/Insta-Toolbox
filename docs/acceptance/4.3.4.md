# 4.3.4 acceptance

Development candidate. Assembly, source and rendered checks passed. Production
browser, sandbox, background, installation, authenticated acceptance and
exact-candidate CI are pending. No official release is recorded here.

## Scope

The preceding 4.3.3 candidate passed its automated and candidate CI gates, but
live testing found that a native Followers dialog could mount before its rows
and scroll container. The primary checker then advanced without reading that
list. Stop preserved the saved comparison and a separate partial export, but
native comparison acceptance failed.

This repair waits for initial native-list readiness for up to 20 seconds.
Visible loading indicators prevent readiness; hidden indicators do not. Exact
small lists can be ready without a scroller, while an exact zero must remain
stable across a rendering turn. Completion still requires fresh exact totals.

Stop interrupts the readiness wait. A loading timeout leaves the current list
open, keeps the saved comparison unchanged, retains observed rows separately
and explains how to retry. The checker cannot advance to the next list after
that timeout. Manual list scans retain their existing default timing.

The exact profile, account, owner, count-control and retained-dialog checks
continue to apply. Native captures keep list-dialog provenance and cannot
become numeric account IDs or authenticated Presence evidence. Growth drafts,
action-specific reviews and the isolated mutation activator retain their
existing boundaries.

## Evidence boundary

The [4.3.3 record](./4.3.3.md) preserves its passing automated checks and observed
live failure. Those results do not validate the new 4.3.4 build. Synthetic
fixtures model selected interactions and do not establish complete Instagram
backend equivalence or authenticated mutation outcomes. Private captures and
exports remain outside the repository.
