Development candidate. Final assembly, source, package safety, production browser,
sandbox, background and rendering checks passed. Installation,

## Scope

Live diagnosis of 4.3.5 found that a music/audio descendant link was interpreted
as a second post ID. This caused the post-Like activity to skip an otherwise
eligible article. The repair matches full canonical `/p/<id>`, `/reel/<id>`
and `/reels/<id>` paths, keeping query strings and fragments separate from path
identity. Canonical aliases with the same ID remain one target.

Audio-only articles and descendant routes cannot establish a post target.
Different canonical IDs remain ambiguous, and duplicate Like controls still
prevent selection. Same-origin checks, exact root/control revalidation, account
binding, reviewed activity authority and verified outcomes remain unchanged.

Saved Growth seed drafts can also fail to restore when Instagram finishes
verifying the signed-in account after startup. The repair retries read-only
restoration once through the existing context observer, bound to the initial
route, exact account and the draft's fresh Following basis/digest. Edits, new
work, busy state, route changes or evidence drift cancel it. Restoration never
reads seed lists, starts Follow or restores action approval automatically.
