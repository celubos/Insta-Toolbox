## History storage boundary

The approved repair design records independent immutable changes in the
userscript's private manager storage. A change is confirmed only after its
write is acknowledged. Discovery builds the confirmed view from records this
tab has received; it may lag other tabs and is not a globally fresh account
history. This implemented boundary passed component checks; full same-build
and installed validation remain pending.

Clear excludes observed activity and older or concurrent activity waiting to
sync when it is later discovered. A tab can record new activity after it has
observed that Clear. Clear remains explicit and bound to the verified account;
it does not grant action authority or promise physical erasure of all storage.
The private legacy V1 backup is retained; later legacy imports are quarantined
after Clear rather than restoring cleared history.

The displayed log and export remain limited to 500 entries. Cleanup work is
bounded to batches of up to 32 records; this is not a hard cap on every
physical key across tabs. Private backing records can outnumber visible rows.

The metadata adds only `GM.getValue`, `GM.setValue`, `GM.listValues` and
`GM.deleteValue`, retaining every legacy grant. These access this userscript's
private manager storage. No network, cookie, connection or domain permission
is added. Identity, author, stable URLs and action review boundaries remain
unchanged.
