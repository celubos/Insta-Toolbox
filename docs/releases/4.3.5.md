# Insta Toolbox 4.3.5

- Start native list collection when verified rows and their scroll container
  are available, even while a trailing pagination loader is mounted.
- Account for dialog, overflow and viewport clipping when checking loader
  visibility. Genuinely visible loading still prevents unproven completion.
- Retain native loading timeouts as partial Growth draft evidence without
  creating candidates or action authority. Preserve exact empty-list checks,
  fresh ending totals, Stop and manual scan defaults.

This is a development candidate. Final-build source and full automated checks,
installation, authenticated acceptance and CI are pending. Version 4.3.4 was
not officially released after its clipped-loader readiness failure.

Tampermonkey remains the active update channel. Publication is limited to the
tested userscript, SBOM and checksums. Extension, desktop and web/PWA downloads
remain on hold at 4.3.1, with all package and Pages gates preserved.

See [acceptance](../acceptance/4.3.5.md) and
[compatibility](../compatibility/4.3.5.md) for scope and remaining checks.
