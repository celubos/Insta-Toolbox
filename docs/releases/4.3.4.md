# Insta Toolbox 4.3.4

- Wait for native Followers and Following lists to finish initial loading
  before scanning. A mounted dialog alone does not establish an empty list.
- Keep Stop responsive during that bounded wait. A loading timeout preserves
  the saved comparison, retains observed rows separately and offers retry
  guidance without advancing to the next list.
- Ignore hidden loading indicators while retaining exact owner, account,
  dialog and fresh ending-count checks. Manual scan defaults remain unchanged.

This is a development candidate. Final-build automated checks, installation,
authenticated acceptance and CI are pending. The preceding 4.3.3 candidate was
not officially released after its native-list readiness failure.

Tampermonkey remains the active update channel. Publication is limited to the
tested userscript, SBOM and checksums. Extension, desktop and web/PWA downloads
remain on hold at 4.3.1, and their package and Pages gates remain required.

See [acceptance](../acceptance/4.3.4.md) and
[compatibility](../compatibility/4.3.4.md) for scope and remaining checks.
